# Batch 10 design rules

- Six compositions use one evaluated donor BDDL and its five initial states byte-for-byte.
- Components may be combined only when every object, fixture, and native target region already exists in that donor scene.
- Stove/package and plate-push pairs are evaluated in both orders; trajectory alignment remains a soft prior.
- Caddy objects occupy distinct left/back compartments; both orders are retained.
- The caddy scene is exact ADAPT_119. ANLGX_176 contributes the mug-to-back semantic primitive, but its alternative black-book-slot mug pose is not copied because ADAPT_119's salad already occupies that slot.
- Every ordered sequence is absent from all indexed tasks and reserved Batch-8/9 source definitions.
- Strict ordered native events plus final BDDL define success; Compose final completion has no TC threshold.
